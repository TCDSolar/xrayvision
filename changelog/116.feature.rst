Add Bayesian visibility fitting for existing circular, elliptical, loop, and mixed-source models through `~xrayvision.vis_forward_fit.bayesian.BayesianVisibilityProblem`, with explicit unit-aware priors and a normalised complex-visibility likelihood. Support emcee posterior sampling with marginal credible intervals and sampler diagnostics, and dynesty nested sampling with weighted posterior samples and evidence estimates for model comparison. Add log likelihood, AIC, and BIC to shared fit statistics. Extend the documentation and examples with synthetic parameter recovery, comparison of overlapping circular sources against an elliptical source, weighted corner plots, and Bayesian fitting of STIX and RHESSI observations.
