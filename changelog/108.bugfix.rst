Fix `~xrayvision.clean.ms_clean` producing incorrect flux when run with more than one scale, and avoid repeating a convolution unnecessarily when precomputing the scale cross-terms.
Fix incorrect centering of the dirty beam and scale kernels in `~xrayvision.clean.clean` and `~xrayvision.clean.ms_clean` for even-sized maps or beams.
